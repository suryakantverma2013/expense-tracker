Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name and email address when you register.
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data: basic information about how you interact with the app, such as pages visited and actions taken.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to provide and improve the Spendly service:

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To display your expense history and summaries.
  • To improve the performance and reliability of the application.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties. We may use trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting and database services) who process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality obligations. These providers do not have independent access to your data.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by closing your account.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the information provided on the site. We will respond to all requests within a reasonable timeframe.
